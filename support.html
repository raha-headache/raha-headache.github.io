01Using Raha

  • History: review, edit or delete headache, medication and daily entries.
  • Account › Privacy & Data: export your diary and manage the copy on this device.
  • Secure Sync: choose Automatic, Manual/Sync Now, or disconnect.
  • Reminders: manage choices in the app and notification/Live Activity permissions in iOS Settings.

02Exporting your data

Account › Privacy & Data › Export My Data creates an on-device ZIP containing JSON, CSV and a guide. It includes records present on that device. If you sync across devices, refresh sync first for their latest changes.

The portable ZIP and doctor-report PDFs are not encrypted. They are shared/saved only to destinations you select through iOS. Keep them carefully; Raha cannot delete copies you save or share elsewhere.

03Remove from device vs disconnect vs cloud deletion

  • Disconnect sync stops future transfers while preserving local and existing cloud records.
  • Remove from This Device clears this device’s diary and signs you out; cloud records remain.
  • Deleting an individual record can synchronize as a deletion marker, with metadata retained to propagate it.
  • Uninstalling is not account deletion and does not erase cloud data, external exports or Apple backups.
Full cloud-account deletion is not yet available in the current app. Contact Raha about a cloud-data or account deletion request. Removing data from this iPhone does not delete the cloud copy.

See the Privacy Policy for retention and request details.

04Privacy, data requests and general support

Contact Raha for access, correction, deletion and other privacy requests, or for help using the app. Email links open your mail app; this website does not upload your diary or submit a request automatically.

05What to include when you contact us

Include your app version, iOS version and a short issue description if useful. Do not send health details, diary attachments, passwords, identity documents or payment information. Raha does not automatically attach device diagnostics or health records to support messages.

06Subscriptions

If a subscription is offered, Apple handles its billing and management. Use Apple subscriptions to manage/cancel, or Report a Problem for refund requests. Deleting the app or records does not cancel billing.

07Medical purpose

Raha is a personal diary to help you discuss recorded information with clinicians. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dose recommendations or emergency monitoring. Seek local emergency care if you believe you have a medical emergency.
