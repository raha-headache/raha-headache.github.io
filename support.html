01Using Raha

  • History — review, edit or delete any headache, medication or daily entry.
  • Account › Privacy & Data — export your data and manage what happens to your diary on this device.
  • Secure Sync — optional. Choose Automatic (saved changes are sent when connectivity allows) or Manual (changes wait until you tap Sync Now), or disconnect.
  • Reminders — set them in the app; iOS Settings controls notification permissions, lock-screen detail and Live Activities.

02Connect Apple Health

Open Account › Apple Health, select sleep, steps or water and tap Connect Apple Health. In Apple’s permission sheet, choose what Raha may read. Your shared records appear in Today and the Health record browser, with charts and available history. Records depend on your device, sources and permissions; water must already have been logged in Health or a connected app.

If no shared records appear, check that Health has records for the selected month and review Raha’s app access in Health. Missing records are not zero. Manage connection changes Raha’s requested types; disconnect stops reading without deleting anything in Health. Imported Health values remain separate from manual check-ins and are not included in current Raha doctor reports, diary exports or cloud sync.

03Exporting your data

Account › Privacy & Data › Export My Data creates a ZIP file on your iPhone containing JSON and CSV files and a short guide.

The export includes only the records present on that device. If you use Secure Sync on several devices, refresh sync first so updates from your other devices are included.

The ZIP is portable and not encrypted. It is shared only when you choose a recipient or save destination with iOS — store it carefully. Copies you save or share are outside Raha’s deletion control.

04Remove from device vs disconnect vs cloud deletion

  • Disconnect sync — stops future transfers. Your records stay on this device and your existing cloud records stay in the cloud.
  • Remove from This Device — clears the diary from this device and signs you out. Your cloud data is preserved.
  • Deleting a record — removes it in the app; with sync on, the deletion travels to your other devices as a deletion marker, and some metadata can remain to do that.
  • Uninstalling the app — removes the app from this device. It is not account deletion and does not delete cloud data.

You can request deletion of your Raha cloud account and its diary data from Account › Privacy & Data › Delete Account. Confirm the request using Sign in with Apple. We complete received requests within two days. The app shows the request status and confirms when deletion is complete.

You can cancel from the pending-deletion screen until processing starts. The two-day completion window is not a guaranteed waiting period: processing may start sooner, and cancellation is no longer available once it starts.

While a request is pending, cloud access for that account is blocked. Records already on your iPhone remain available. After the app confirms completion, use Remove remaining device data to clear that device’s diary and finish the reset. Other devices, Apple Health records, device backups and files you exported or shared are separate copies.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in your Apple subscriptions settings.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.

05Privacy, data requests and general support

Start account deletion in the app so you can verify ownership and follow its status. Contact us for help, access or correction requests, or other privacy questions. Use general support for anything else.

06What to include when you contact us

If useful, include your app version, your iOS version and a short description of the issue.

Please avoid health details, diary attachments, passwords and payment information. Your app version, iOS version and a short description usually give us the best starting point.

07Subscriptions

Raha Premium is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription, billed and managed by Apple. The app shows your local price, billing period and included features before you purchase.

Open the gear in Account to restore purchases or manage your subscription, or use Apple subscriptions settings. Deleting the app or your Raha account does not cancel a subscription.

08Medical purpose

Raha is a personal diary for keeping records and discussing them with clinicians. It does not diagnose, treat or predict disease, identify causes, recommend medication doses or provide emergency monitoring. If you believe you have a medical emergency, seek local emergency care.
