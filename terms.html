02Purpose and medical boundary

Raha is a personal headache diary for keeping records, descriptive summaries and reports to discuss with a clinician. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose, treat or predict disease, identify proven causes, recommend medication doses, or provide emergency monitoring. No clinician–patient relationship is created.

Patterns describe associations within your entered records and may be incomplete. Consult a qualified clinician before medical decisions or medication changes.

If you believe you have a medical emergency, seek local emergency care immediately.

04Local use and optional sync

Local use requires no sign-in. Optional sync depends on consent, connectivity and provider availability. Automatic mode sends saved changes when connectivity allows; Manual waits for Sync Now; Disconnect stops transfers and retains local and existing cloud copies.

Review upload and recovery confirmations. Sync can be delayed and conflicts may require your review. Unconfirmed uploads are not a reliable recovery copy. Uninstalling or signing out does not delete your cloud data.

Full cloud-account deletion is not yet available in the current app. See Support and the Privacy Policy for the current limits and request route.

05Reports, reminders and limitations

Reports summarize self-entered records; they are not certified clinical records or diagnoses. Review their content and the recipient before sharing.

Reminders and Live Activities can be delayed or unavailable because of iOS permissions, settings or device state. They are not emergency monitoring or a medication-safety system.

Files exported or shared outside Raha are under your chosen storage/recipient’s control. Deleting source records cannot recall those copies.
