02Purpose and medical boundary

Raha is a personal headache diary that provides descriptive summaries and reports of what you record. It is not medical advice, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, it is not an emergency service, and using it does not create a clinician–patient relationship.

Patterns show associations within the records you entered. They are not proven causes and they do not predict future headaches.

Consult a qualified clinician before making medical decisions or changing any medication.

If you believe you have a medical emergency, seek local emergency care immediately.

You do not need to sign in to use Raha on your device. Optional Secure Sync depends on your consent, your connectivity and the availability of service providers.

Pay attention to upload and recovery confirmations. Sync can be delayed, and conflicting edits need your review. Do not rely on changes that have not been confirmed as uploaded, and do not assume that uninstalling the app or signing out deletes your cloud data.
