01Who we are and scope

Raha (“we”, “us”) operates the Raha headache diary app for iPhone and this informational website. This policy describes the current app: information kept locally, optional Secure Sync, support messages and visits to this website.

For privacy questions and data requests, contact appraha4@gmail.com or use our Support page. Other services you choose to use have their own privacy policies.

02Information you choose to record

Depending on the features you use, your diary can contain headache timing, duration, severity, symptoms, possible triggers and notes; medication names, use and response; headache-free confirmations; optional daily sleep, stress and water entries; and your onboarding, tracking and app preferences.

Optional doctor-report details can include a name or patient label, birth year, a diagnosis or appointment reason you enter, an appointment date and a note for your clinician. You decide what to enter; avoid recording other people’s information without authority.

We use these records to save and edit your diary, calculate descriptive Patterns, prepare reports you request and operate your chosen reminders. The summaries describe your entries; they do not establish medical causes or provide a diagnosis.

03Local storage on your iPhone

Local use does not require a Raha cloud account. Without Secure Sync, your diary works on your device and is not uploaded to Raha. Protection of the local copy depends on iOS and your device security settings.

Deleting the app or erasing its data may remove your only working diary copy. Apple device or computer backups depend on your own settings and are separate from Raha Secure Sync. We do not promise that Raha data is excluded from those backups.

04Optional account and Secure Sync

Secure Sync is optional and is explained before the first upload. It uses Sign in with Apple, Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check. Firebase starts after sync consent or when resuming an already linked diary.

Sync processes your Apple authentication identifier, an email or private relay address if supplied by Apple/Firebase, a Firebase account identifier, the diary content and report identity/settings selected for sync, record and device identifiers, revisions, timestamps and deletion markers. These support authentication, owner access, synchronization, conflict review and recovery you choose.

In Automatic mode, saved changes are sent when connectivity allows. In Manual mode they wait for Sync Now. Disconnecting stops future transfers but retains both your local diary and existing cloud records. Language, appearance, notification permissions and temporary report files are device-local.

Apple and Google also process technical and security information such as IP addresses, app/device/request information, attestation material and tokens under their service policies. You enter your Apple password only in Apple’s sign-in interface; Raha receives an authentication token, not that password. Signing in is not consent to unrelated marketing.

05Sharing and service providers

Google Firebase provides optional authentication, cloud storage and integrity checks. Apple provides sign-in, operating-system/device features and App Store billing where offered. Email/support providers process the minimum information needed to deliver and answer messages you send.

Reports and exports go only to recipients or save destinations that you select through iOS. Providers process information to deliver and secure their services under applicable terms, contractual safeguards and legal obligations. We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and service security.

The current app has no advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. We do not sell health data, use it for targeted advertising, track you across apps, or put diary health values in analytics or diagnostic logs.

Provider information: Firebase privacy, Apple Privacy Policy and Sign in with Apple & Privacy.

06Retention and deletion

Local entries stay until you edit or remove them. Erasing local data or using Remove from This Device does not erase cloud records, Apple backups, or files already exported or shared. Disconnecting or uninstalling is not account deletion.

Cloud records persist for your linked diary; no automatic expiry is promised. Deleting an individual record can sync a deletion marker (“tombstone”), and some metadata may remain so the deletion reaches other devices.

Full cloud-account deletion is not yet available in the current app. Contact Raha about a cloud-data or account deletion request. Removing data from this iPhone does not delete the cloud copy.

This is a request handled by a person, not an automated deletion feature. Do not send medical records, Apple passwords, identity documents or full diary exports with your request.

07Your choices and rights

You can edit/delete records in History; use Account › Privacy & Data › Export My Data; disconnect sync or choose Manual/Automatic; remove this device’s diary; and control tracking modules, reminders and lock-screen visibility. Disabling a tracking module preserves existing records.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access/a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, withdraw consent for optional processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent stops future optional processing and does not undo earlier lawful processing.

Processing supports the service you request, account security, your consent for optional sync (including explicit consent for sensitive diary information where required), responses to support requests and legal obligations. We may need to verify a request using the minimum necessary information; this website has no sensitive-data submission form.

08Security and international processing

We use reasonable access controls and Firebase’s protections for data in transit and storage. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized providers and developer administration can process cloud content. No system is risk-free.

Optional cloud diary storage is in Google’s United States multi-region. Authentication, security and support infrastructure may process information elsewhere under provider arrangements and applicable safeguards. We do not promise that all information stays in your country.

09Notifications, exports and device privacy

Reminders are local and subject to iOS permissions. Lock-screen wording is generic by default; your choices govern additional detail. An ongoing recording Live Activity can reveal recording status and a timer. You can control these features in the app and iOS Settings.

Data exports are created on-device as ZIP files containing JSON/CSV and a guide. They contain records available on that device; refresh sync first for other-device updates. Doctor reports are generated on-device as PDFs. Files are shared or saved only through destinations you select.

Portable exports and PDFs are not encrypted. Store/share them carefully. Removing source records cannot recall external copies; those recipients and storage services follow their own policies.

10This website and cookies

This informational website has no login, health-data collection form, advertising cookies or added marketing analytics. Your selected language is stored in browser local storage.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they sign in; other connection/device information may be processed under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not upload your app diary to GitHub. Never send medical records through the site.

11Adults and children

Raha is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. If you believe a child provided personal information through cloud sync or support, contact us so we can assess and address it under applicable law.
