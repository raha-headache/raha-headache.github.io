01Who we are and scope

Raha (“Raha”, “we”, “us”) operates the Raha headache diary app for iPhone and this informational legal website. In this policy, “you” means the person using the app or visiting this site.

This policy explains the information processed locally on your iPhone, through optional Secure Sync, when you contact us, and when you browse this website. It does not cover other apps, websites or services that you choose to use alongside Raha, which have their own policies.

For privacy questions or support, contact Raha using the details on our Support page.

02Information you choose to record

Raha processes the diary information you choose to enter. If you connect Apple Health, it also reads the selected records as explained below. Your manual diary can include:

  • headache timing, duration and severity, symptoms, possible triggers and your notes;
  • medication names, when you used them and how you felt they worked;
  • confirmations of headache-free days;
  • optional daily sleep, stress and water entries;
  • your onboarding and tracking choices and app preferences;
  • optional doctor-report identity details, such as a name or patient label, birth year, a diagnosis or reason you type in, an appointment date and a note for your clinician.

We use these entries to save and edit your records, calculate descriptive summaries in Patterns, prepare doctor reports when you ask for one, and run the reminders you choose to set.

You decide what to enter. Please avoid recording information about other people unless you have the authority to do so.

03Your Apple Health records

You can connect Apple Health to show available sleep, step counts and water intake in Raha. Browse your daily records, charts and available history alongside your diary. You choose the types to request, and Apple asks what Raha may read. This integration is read-only and does not change records in Health.

Raha reads the types you selected while the app is active. Imported values and source names are held in memory for display; they are not saved in Raha’s diary database, sent through Secure Sync, included in usage analytics or transmitted to Raha’s servers. Only your device-local choice of types is saved. Imported values are cleared when the app leaves the foreground, when you disconnect or when you remove Raha’s data from this device. Your original records remain in Health under Apple’s controls.

Imported Health values are separate from your manual sleep, stress and water answers. They do not replace those answers or establish whether you had a headache. Current doctor reports and raw diary exports contain your Raha entries and do not include the imported Health values.

Available records depend on your Health sources, device and sharing permissions. An empty result can mean that no records exist or that access was not shared; Raha does not present missing values as zero. For sleep, Raha counts asleep intervals in a local noon-to-noon window labelled by the morning date, merges overlapping intervals, and shows one source per window. Its displayed totals can differ from other apps. These are descriptive records, not a diagnosis or proof of a headache cause.

Account › Apple Health › Manage connection lets you change Raha’s choices or disconnect. Disconnecting stops Raha from reading and clears its displayed values; it does not revoke Apple’s permissions or delete records in Health. Review or revoke sharing in the Health app by opening your profile and finding Raha under app access. Apple’s management of your Health records and any Health syncing is separate from Raha Secure Sync.

04Local storage on your iPhone

Without Secure Sync, the diary works on your device. You do not need a Raha cloud account, and your diary is not uploaded to Raha.

How safely local data is stored depends on iOS and on your device’s security, such as your passcode and Face ID or Touch ID settings. If you remove the app or erase its data, you may remove your only working copy of the diary.

Device backups, such as iCloud Backup or computer backups, are controlled by your Apple account and iOS choices. They are separate from Raha Secure Sync, and we cannot promise that Raha data is excluded from them.

Optional App Lock uses iOS authentication with Face ID or Touch ID and device-passcode fallback. Raha receives an authentication result, not biometric templates or your passcode. It also covers the diary when the app leaves the foreground. App Lock protects access on this device; it does not erase cloud records or shared copies.

05Optional account and Secure Sync

Secure Sync is optional. It starts after your separate consent in the app and Sign in with Apple, or resumes for a diary you already linked. Google’s cloud services provide account authentication, diary storage and app integrity checks. App update checks and optional usage analytics are separate, as explained below.

When you use Secure Sync, we process:

  • your Apple authentication identifier, and your email address or Apple private relay address if supplied by the sign-in service;
  • your Raha cloud account identifier;
  • your synced diary content and saved report identity details and settings;
  • record and device identifiers, revision information, timestamps and deletion markers.

This information is used to authenticate you, give you access to your own diary, synchronise records across your devices, help you review conflicting edits and support recovery that you choose.

In Automatic mode, saved changes are sent when connectivity allows. In Manual mode, changes wait until you tap Sync Now. Disconnecting stops future transfers and keeps both the records on your device and the records already in the cloud.

Apple and Google also process security and technical data — such as IP addresses, request, device and app information, attestation material and tokens — under their own service policies. Your Apple password is entered only into Apple’s sign-in interface. Raha receives an authentication token, not your Apple password.

Signing in does not mean you agree to marketing. We do not infer any unrelated marketing consent from your use of Secure Sync.

06App update checks

Raha automatically checks app-version settings using Google’s cloud configuration service while the app is active. These functional checks are separate from optional usage analytics and Secure Sync; they can run without a cloud account or an Analytics opt-in.

Google’s installation and cloud configuration services process an installation identifier and app/device configuration metadata, including app and SDK versions, operating-system information, language, time zone and country information, to deliver settings and operate the service. No diary entries, cloud sign-in identity or subscription information are supplied by Raha for these checks.

Settings can recommend an update or require one for an older version. Failed checks or an offline state do not block local diary access. Update checks do not grant usage-analytics consent, enable advertising tracking or upload your health records.

07Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is a separate, optional choice in setup and Account › Privacy & Data. It is off until you turn on “Help improve Raha”. You can use the diary and Secure Sync without enabling analytics.

If you opt in, Google Analytics processes generic app activity, such as app opens, first opens, sessions and engagement, together with a random installation identifier and basic app, operating-system, language and device information. Google derives general location, such as country or region, from the connection’s IP address. Raha does not request location access for analytics.

These reports help us understand app usage and retention. They do not include your headache records, symptoms, medicines, notes, doctor reports or cloud sign-in identity. We do not set an Analytics User-ID, upload diary values, or report which clinical screens you visit. Installation-based usage reports are not anonymous data and are not an exact count of individual people.

Advertising storage, ad personalization and ad-user-data consent remain denied. Raha does not collect IDFA or IDFV for analytics, include an advertising SDK, or use cross-app advertising tracking. We do not link Analytics to Google Ads or use health-related audiences.

Turning this choice off stops future usage reporting and clears the Analytics data held locally by the SDK. It does not automatically erase reports already sent to Google. Retention of those reports depends on the Google Analytics property settings and provider policies. Contact support for privacy requests; do not send medical records or credentials.

Google’s cloud authentication, sync and app integrity services may separately process technical diagnostics needed to operate and maintain their services. We do not intentionally put diary values in those logs. Raha does not include a separate automatic crash-reporting service.

08Sharing and service providers

We use a small number of service providers:

  • Google — cloud account authentication, diary storage, app integrity checks, functional update settings and separately consented usage analytics. Google cloud services privacy information · Google Analytics data disclosures.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, operating-system notification and device features, and App Store purchases where offered. Apple Privacy Policy · Sign in with Apple and privacy.
  • If you email us, the minimum necessary email and support providers process your message so that we can reply.

Doctor reports and data exports go only to the recipients or save destinations that you select using iOS sharing.

Service providers process data to provide and secure their services under their applicable terms, contractual data-protection safeguards and legal obligations.

We may disclose information where the law requires it, or where reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or the security of people and the service. We do not sell health data and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

09Retention and deletion

Local entries stay on your device until you edit or remove them. Erasing local data or removing the diary from a device does not erase cloud records, Apple device backups, or copies you have already exported or shared.

Cloud data persists while it is kept for your linked diary. There is no automatic expiry guarantee. When you delete a record in the app, the deletion can sync as a deletion marker (a “tombstone”), and some metadata can remain so that the deletion reaches your other devices. Disconnecting sync or deleting the app is not account deletion.

You can request deletion of your Raha cloud account and its diary data from Account › Privacy & Data › Delete Account. Confirm the request using Sign in with Apple. We complete received requests within two days. The app shows the request status and confirms when deletion is complete.

You can cancel from the pending-deletion screen until processing starts. The two-day completion window is not a guaranteed waiting period: processing may start sooner, and cancellation is no longer available once it starts.

While a request is pending, cloud access for that account is blocked. Records already on your iPhone remain available. After the app confirms completion, use Remove remaining device data to clear that device’s diary and finish the reset. Other devices, Apple Health records, device backups and files you exported or shared are separate copies.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in your Apple subscriptions settings.

After account deletion, a restricted account identifier and request record are kept for at least 48 hours to block old sign-in tokens and complete security cleanup. A completion receipt without your account identity may be retained so an offline device can later confirm the result. It contains a random receipt identifier, status and completion time, not your diary.

Provider security logs and residual backups follow the providers’ retention and deletion rules. Deleting the active Raha account does not mean that every provider backup is erased immediately. See Google cloud services privacy information.

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, for security and to meet legal obligations. For help with a deletion request, contact Raha; do not send medical records, passwords or full diary exports.

10Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • edit or delete records in History;
  • export your data from Account › Privacy & Data › Export My Data;
  • disconnect Secure Sync, or choose Manual or Automatic sync;
  • use Remove from This Device;
  • control tracking modules, reminders and lock-screen visibility in the app and in iOS Settings. Turning off a tracking module keeps your existing entries.
  • request cloud-account deletion in the app, view its status and cancel before processing starts;

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to request access to or a copy of your information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, to withdraw consent for optional processing, and to complain to your local data-protection regulator. Withdrawing consent stops future optional processing; it does not undo processing that was lawful before.

We may need to verify a request. We will ask only for the minimum information needed, and this website never asks you to submit sensitive content.

We process information where necessary to provide your account and keep it secure; on the basis of your consent for optional Secure Sync (including explicit consent for sensitive diary information where the law requires it); to respond to support requests; and to comply with legal obligations. We do not claim that this policy meets the specific requirements of every country.

11Security and international processing

We use access controls and Google’s cloud protections for data in transit and storage. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted: authorised service providers and Raha administrators can process cloud content. No system can guarantee complete security.

Optional cloud diary storage is located in Google’s United States multi-region. Authentication, security and support infrastructure may process information in other countries, subject to the providers’ arrangements and applicable safeguards. We do not claim that all data stays in your country.

12Notifications, exports and device privacy

Reminders are local notifications subject to your iOS permissions. By default, lock-screen wording is generic; your settings decide whether more detail is shown. While a headache is being recorded, an ongoing Live Activity can reveal that recording is in progress and show a timer.

You can change notification and Live Activity permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Doctor-report PDFs and data exports are shared only when you choose a recipient or save destination. These files are portable and not encrypted, so store and share them carefully. Deleting the source records in Raha cannot recall copies that already exist elsewhere.

13This website and cookies

This legal website has no login, no form for collecting health data, no advertising cookies and no added marketing analytics. It stores your selected language in your browser so the site remembers it.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. Other connection and device information may be processed under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not send your app diary to GitHub.

Please never upload or send medical records through this website.

14Adults and children

Raha is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Secure Sync or a support message, please contact us so we can assess and address it under applicable law.

15Updates and contact

If we make material changes, we will update this policy and its date and, where required, give appropriate notice or ask for consent.
